## 🚀 My Main Projects

### 💰 [Lunoss Pay - Crypto Payments Made Simple](https://github.com/LunossPay)

**🎯 The Problem:**  
Brazilian startups want to accept cryptocurrency, but integration is complex, expensive, and requires deep blockchain knowledge. There's no simple API with multi-wallet support and automatic payment distribution.

**💡 The Solution:**  
A payment API for Solana that makes accepting crypto as easy as using Stripe. Startups can integrate with 3 lines of code and automatically split payments across multiple wallets (founders, investors, employees, contractors).

**📊 Market Opportunity:**  
- Crypto payments in Brazil growing **150% year-over-year**
- 30% of Brazilian startups want to accept crypto but don't have the technical capability
- Stripe and Coinbase don't offer localized Web3 solutions for Latin America

**💰 Business Model:**  
- Transaction fee: **1-2%** per processed payment
- Target: Process **R$1M/month** = **R$10-20k MRR**
- Second vertical: White-label solution for fintechs and payment processors

**🛠️ Tech Stack:**  
`Solana` `Web3.js` `Node.js` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL`

**🔥 Why it matters:**  
Web3 is the future of payments, but it's still too complicated for 99% of companies. This bridges the gap between crypto's potential and real-world usability.

**📈 Current Status:**  
MVP in development. Targeting first pilot customers by Q2 2025.

### 🎵 [Emotify - Understand Your Emotions Through Music](https://github.com/ArthurDS-tech/Emotify-Spotify)

**🎯 The Problem:**  
Mental health is a global crisis. **80% of people don't understand their emotional patterns**, and traditional mental health apps require manual input, discipline, and effort that most users don't have.

**💡 The Solution:**  
An emotional wellness app that analyzes your Spotify listening habits to automatically map your emotional patterns over time. No manual input. No invasive questions. Just insights based on what you're already doing — listening to music.

**📊 Market Opportunity:**  
- Digital mental health market: **$6 billion globally** (growing 25% annually)
- Spotify has **500 million users worldwide**, 70 million in Brazil alone
- Competitors (Headspace, Calm, BetterHelp) require active engagement — Emotify is passive

**💰 Business Model:**  
- **Freemium:** Free basic emotional reports
- **Premium:** R$19.90/month for advanced insights, mood tracking, and personalized recommendations
- Target: **10,000 users** in first year = **R$200k ARR** at 10% conversion

**🛠️ Tech Stack:**  
`JavaScript` `React` `Spotify API` `Python` `Data Analytics` `Machine Learning`

**🔥 Why it matters:**  
Everyone listens to music. This turns passive listening into actionable emotional intelligence without requiring users to change their behavior.

**📈 Current Status:**  
Beta version with real users testing. Refining emotion categorization algorithms based on feedback.

### 💬 [WhatsApp Financial Copilot - AI Finance for Freelancers](https://github.com/ArthurDS-tech/WhatsApp-Financial-Copilot)

**🎯 The Problem:**  
**15 million MEIs (micro-entrepreneurs)** in Brazil are drowning in financial chaos. They don't understand cash flow, taxes, or profitability. Traditional finance apps are too complex and require discipline they don't have.

**💡 The Solution:**  
A WhatsApp bot powered by AI that manages finances through natural conversation. User texts: *"Gastei R$200 no mercado"* → System automatically categorizes, alerts about overspending, and projects cash flow for the month.

**📊 Market Opportunity:**  
- **15 million MEIs** in Brazil (growing 20% annually)
- Financial management market for SMBs: **R$2 billion** in Brazil
- Competitors (Conta Azul, Nibo) use complex dashboards — 80% of MEIs never log in after first week
- **WhatsApp has 99% penetration** in Brazil — users are already there

**💰 Business Model:**  
- **R$29/month** per MEI (affordable entry point)
- Goal: **1,000 paying customers** = **R$29k MRR** in first year
- Unit economics: **R$5 CAC** (WhatsApp virality) vs **R$348 LTV** (12-month retention)

**🛠️ Tech Stack:**  
`Node.js` `Twilio WhatsApp API` `Claude AI` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL`

**🔥 Why it matters:**  
MEIs are the backbone of Brazil's economy, but they're invisible to traditional finance companies. This meets them where they already are — on WhatsApp — and speaks their language.

**📈 Current Status:**  
MVP ready. Starting closed beta with 50 real MEIs to validate retention and willingness to pay.
